Multidisciplinary composer, producer, and programmer blending technology and musical expression. Experienced in scoring for multimedia with a strong foundation in audio DSP, live sound, and software development. Skilled in Max/MSP, Pure Data, and C++, with a keen interest in experimental sound design and developing custom instruments. Contributing innovative audio solutions in media, interactive, and research-driven environments.

Creating emotional, detailed soundscapes and immersive audio experiences, with a portfolio of original music spanning film, dance, theater, and interactive installations. Blending drones, pulsing rhythms, spectral techniques, and aleatoric elements, combining organic spontaneity with careful arrangement.
